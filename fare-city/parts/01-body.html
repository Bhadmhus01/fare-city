How to hustle

Three things and you are a conductor.
1
Stop inside the yellow box to load Yellow box = bus stop. Brake to a full stop inside it. Passengers jump in. Fill up before you move.
2
Drop them at their stop to get paid Every passenger has a destination shown on the left. Stop there and they pay. Skip it and conductor vex — half fare, no tip.
3
Press horn for OWA! Honk near riders, hawkers and pedestrians to scatter them and build combo. Every few calls the multiplier climbs.
4
Swipe up to hop Hop potholes, speed bumps and barriers. One press, bus jumps. Potholes on the ground hurt the bus and slow you down.
5
Horn the crew off your stop Every city has its own people who work the stops — agberos in Lagos, matatu touts in Nairobi, dabbawalas in Mumbai. While they hold the yellow box they take a slice of every fare and nobody boards. Horn them off, or wait them out, and the stop is yours.
6
Ride out city conditions Rain in Lagos, khamsin dust in Cairo, monsoon in Mumbai, Songkran in Bangkok, a Tube strike in London. Each one changes grip, traffic, visibility or how many people want your bus, for about twenty seconds. The radio always warns you.
7
Collect your city's signature One bonus only local drivers can earn. Full house in Cairo, rank discipline in Cape Town, express run in New York. Open a city and read its signature card.
8
Obey the checkpoint LASTMA / police / wardens stop you at checkpoints. Brake inside the pink box for one second, they wave you through. Blow past and it is a fine.
